== Trace sweep, 2026-10-29 (release build at 0c1dae31)

sweep.sh runs each model x mode once unprofiled (timing) and once under
`tools/prof/prof.py run`: IQ1_M resident and IQ2_XXS with --stream-experts;
pp2048 (`gufo bench -p 2048 -n 0 -r 2`) and `gufo prompt` on code.txt (96
prompt tokens, 256 generated, --think off) greedy (-t 0) or sampled (model
preset, -s 1), AR or DSpark (Lucebox Q2K/Q4K drafter). Each process starts
with a cold expert cache. rollup.py: GPU busy, wall span and stages per
decoded token (per pp2048 pair of repetitions for pp); shapes.py: per-shape
launch costs; gaps.py: idle time between kernels by size; bytes.py: weight
bytes read per decoded token from the GGUF headers.

Measured ceilings (tools/bench/gfx1151_peak.hip): DRAM read 239.8 GB/s,
write 220.2, copy 210.2; WMMA FP16 55.4 TFLOP/s, BF16 55.4, INT8 57.1 TOPS;
VALU FP32 FMA 26.5 TFLOP/s; LDS read 6.5 TB/s.

Weights per decoded token (bytes.py, Engram table rows excluded):
IQ1_M 7,147 MiB -> 32.0 tok/s at 240 GB/s; IQ2_XXS 7,408 MiB -> 30.9 tok/s.
Attention projections 3,646 MiB, six routed experts 1,707 / 1,968 MiB,
shared expert 928, output head 434, Engram projection 206, router 150,
hyper-connections 75.

Single-token bandwidth (IQ1_M AR, shapes.py): output head 455 MB in 1908.5
us (238 GB/s), output_a 23.07 MB in 99.6 us (232), q_b 28.84 MB in 127.2
(227), shared gate+up 16.22 MB in 73.6 (220), q_a+kv 9.75 MB in 45.9 (212),
output_b 28.84 MB in 139.9 (206), shared down 8.11 MB in 39.3 (206); routed
gate+up IQ1_S 27.6 MB in 135.8 (203), IQ2_XXS layers 31 MB in 180.8 (171),
routed down IQ1_S 13.8 MB in 80.6 (171), IQ1_M 15.5 MB in 89.0 (174); BF16

DSpark verification: ~4.2 rows per step touch 24-36 distinct experts per
layer; the grouped expert launches read at 188-210 GB/s, so a step's ~88 ms
of reads matches the ~85-90 ms of kernels. 5-6-row output heads take the
lane kernel (2,426 us, 98 calls) because the split scratch holds no
129,280-row head beyond 4 columns.

Idle (gaps.py): AR IQ1_M 2.89 ms/token, 2.35 of it gaps under 5 us; AR
IQ2_XXS 5.37 (1.63 in >1 ms waits for expert reads); DSpark IQ1_M 5.54 ms
per step (~1.2 around the logits copies, 0.27 between the drafter's
quantize_q8_1 and mul_mat_vec_q); DSpark IQ2_XXS 10.20 per step (~2.9
before expert_plan_kernel while misses are read).
